What SafeHer is — and is not

SafeHer is

  • A place for your emergency contacts. A contact can only receive SOS messages after they agree and confirm a code sent to their phone.
  • An SOS button that saves an alert to your account, tries to text your verified contacts and notifies the SafeHer team.
  • A clear status after SOS: you see whether messages are still processing, were accepted by the SMS provider, delivered, or failed — never more than the evidence shows.
  • Helplines and "nearby police / hospital" searches one tap away, and a practice drill so you know the steps.

SafeHer is not

  • An emergency service. It does not contact the police, ambulance or fire service. Call yourself.
  • A guarantee that someone sees a message in time. Phones can be off, out of signal or on silent.
  • A tracker. Your location is read only when you press a button, and shared only if you choose to with an SOS alert.
  • Sending real messages while it runs in test mode: then messages are only simulated, and the app shows a TEST MODE label.

How it works in 3 steps

  1. Sign up with your phone

    Create an account with your phone number and a one-time code. No password to remember.

  2. Add and verify trusted contacts

    Add up to five people. Each one agrees and confirms a code we text to them — only then can they receive an SOS message. Practise once with the SOS drill.

  3. In trouble: call, then SOS

    Call first if you are in danger. Press SOS: the alert is saved, your verified contacts are texted with your location if you choose, and you see the real status.

Helplines in India

Public numbers, answered by public services — not by SafeHer. Tap a number to open your phone's dialer.

    Routing can differ between states; in several states 100 and 1091 are answered through 112.

    Questions

    Does SafeHer call the police for me?

    No. SafeHer never contacts the police, ambulance or fire service. If you are in danger, call yourself — the SOS screen always shows a button for it.

    Who receives my SOS?

    Only the emergency contacts you added who have agreed and verified their number, plus the SafeHer team's admin console (which may not be watched at all times). Your location is included only if you choose to share it.

    Does SafeHer track where I am?

    No. The app reads your location only when you press a button that needs it, and saves it only if you attach it to an SOS alert.

    What does "test mode" mean?

    While SafeHer runs in test mode, SOS messages are simulated and nothing is sent to anyone. The app labels this clearly so it is never mistaken for a real message.
