Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-v1

Development draft. It describes what the software currently does and has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Have it reviewed before any public launch.

What we store

  • Account: your name, your verified phone number, when you registered and last signed in, and the version of these documents you accepted (with the time).
  • Emergency contacts: the names, phone numbers and relationships you add.
  • SOS alerts: the time of each alert, its status and, only if you choose to include it, the location you attached.
  • Incident reports: what you write in a report.

What we never store

Passwords (there are none), one-time codes, or sign-in tokens. Phone verification is handled by Google Firebase Authentication.

Your location

Your browser asks for permission before sharing your location. It is shown on your screen and is only saved if you tick "Include my current location" when creating an SOS alert.

Who can see it

Only you can see your contacts, alerts and reports. The SafeHer administrator can view accounts, alerts and reports to run the service; every such access to a full phone number or to alert coordinates is written to an audit log.

How long we keep it

SOS alert records are kept for 90 days and then become eligible for deletion. You can delete your emergency contacts at any time. Ask the administrator to delete your account and data.

Service providers

Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) processes this data on SafeHer's behalf.
