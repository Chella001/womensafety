        Safety tips

        • Trust your instincts. If a place or person feels wrong, leave and move toward other people.
        • Tell someone your plan. Share where you're going and when you expect to arrive.
        • Keep your phone ready. Charge it, and put your emergency contacts on speed dial.
        • Stay on lit, busy routes at night and keep one ear free of headphones.
        • Know your emergency number. In India, dial 112 for police, fire and ambulance.
