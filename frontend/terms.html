1. What SafeHer is

SafeHer is a personal-safety web application under development. It lets you keep emergency contacts, record SOS alerts, report incidents and view your location on screen.

2. Not an emergency service

SafeHer is not an emergency dispatch service. In its current version an SOS alert is only saved as a record in your account: it does not notify your contacts, the police or anyone else. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number (112 in India) immediately.
